# Working on axomrank-brand-assets

AxomRank brand kit and social media asset library. Part of AxomRank: see README.md for the overview and roadmap.

## Layout

- `brand-kit/v1/`: logos, tokens, fonts and templates
- `illustrations/`: master illustration collection
- `outputs/`, `projects/`, `reel_work/`, `style_work/`: campaign production
- `rights/`: permissions and consent records

## Commands

- `python3 scripts/validate_library.py`

## Rules

- Do not move existing assets, because campaign scripts reference their paths
- Record rights before publishing any asset
- Never present an AI face as the proprietor or the team
- `CAROUSEL_PREFERENCES.md` overrides the original guide (square 1:1, no gamosa borders)
- Commit as the `axomrank` account, in small reviewable commits.
